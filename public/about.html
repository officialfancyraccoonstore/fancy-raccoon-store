What Fancy Raccoon is

Fancy Raccoon is a made-to-order football jersey brand based in India, serving fans in India and Nepal. Every order starts with a kit — a European club such as Real Madrid, Barcelona, Manchester United, Bayern Munich or Juventus, or a national team such as India, Nepal, Argentina, Brazil, Portugal, France, Germany, Spain or England.

From there the jersey becomes yours. You choose the size, add a name and number, and tell us the design you want. If the look already exists in your head, upload reference photos; if it does not, describe it in plain words and our studio works from that. Each brief is reviewed by a person, not a script, and we send a print proof before anything is pressed.

We offer two versions of every kit. The fan-made look-alike is our own recreation: the same colours, sponsor layout and silhouette, made affordable from ₹499. The original/genuine kit is official merchandise, sold at its actual retail price. Fan-made jerseys are our recreations and are not official club products — we say this plainly so there is no confusion.

How an order works

01 · PICK

Choose the club or country, the kit and the version — fan-made or original.

02 · MAKE IT YOURS

Add size, name and number. Describe your design or upload up to six reference photos.

03 · CHECKOUT

Enter your delivery address. Pay cash on delivery, by UPI or bank transfer.

04 · PROOF, THEN PRINT

We send a print proof on WhatsApp. Once you approve it, the jersey is printed and shipped.

Delivery, payment and stock

Shipping is ₹79 and free on orders over ₹1,499. Fan-made jerseys are printed in 5–7 days and arrive in 4–6 days in Indian metros, 7–9 days elsewhere in India and Nepal. Original kits ship from stock once any requested printing is done. We hold 10 units of each jersey, and the site shows what is left.
