Questions

Before you order

How long does a custom jersey take?

Fan-made look-alike jerseys are printed in 5–7 days and delivered in 4–6 days to metros, 7–9 days elsewhere in India and Nepal. Original genuine kits ship from stock quickly once any printing you asked for is done.

Can I send a photo of the exact design I want?

Yes — that is the whole point. Upload up to 6 reference photos in the customiser. They are attached to your order and land directly in our inbox. We reply with a print proof before production.

What does the ₹99 customisation fee cover?

Heat-pressed name and number in your chosen font. If you leave both blank, the fee is removed automatically and you pay the base price.

Do you ship to Nepal?

We do, across all major cities. Shipping is ₹79 on orders under ₹1,499 and free above that. Nepali customers can pay via UPI or cash on delivery.

How do I know my order went through?

The moment you check out, a confirmation appears with your order code and an email lands in your inbox. Every order and reference image is also delivered to officialfancyraccoon.store@gmail.com so our team can start on it immediately.
